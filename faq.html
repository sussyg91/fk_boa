Sign In & Access

Visit the Sign In page and enter your User ID and Password. For this demo, use jimmyregina / jimmyregina2026.
Click "Forgot User ID/Password" on the sign-in page, or visit the recovery page. Enter your registered email address to receive reset instructions.
After multiple failed sign-in attempts, your account may be temporarily locked for security. Use the password recovery page or call us at 1-800-432-1000.

Accounts & Balances

After signing in, go to Account Summary to see all your balances at a glance.
You can view up to 18 months of transactions in Account Activity. For older records, download Online Statements.
Go to Online Statements, select your account and month, then click the PDF button.

Transfers & Payments

Sign in and visit Transfer Funds. Select the source account, enter the destination details and amount, then confirm.
Go to Pay Bills, select your biller, enter the amount and payment date, and confirm. You can also add new billers.
Standard online transfers are limited to $5,000 per day. For higher limits, please call customer service at 1-800-432-1000.

Security & Fraud

We use 256-bit SSL encryption, multi-factor authentication, and 24/7 fraud monitoring to protect your account. Always look for "https://" and the padlock icon in your browser.
Change your password immediately via the password reset page and call our fraud hotline at 1-800-432-1000 right away. Do not share your credentials with anyone.

Mobile Banking

The app offers everything available online: account balances, transaction history, fund transfers, bill pay, statements, and Money Map spending analysis — all on your phone.
After downloading the app and signing in, go to Settings > Alerts & Notifications to customise which events trigger alerts (deposits, low balance, suspicious activity, etc.).
