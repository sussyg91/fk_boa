Privacy Notice

Bank of America is committed to protecting your privacy and the security of your personal information. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information about you when you use our banking services, website, and mobile applications.

By using our services you agree to the terms of this Privacy Notice. We may update this notice periodically — we will notify you of material changes through our website or other communications.

Data We Collect

We collect information about you in order to provide banking services. Categories of information collected include:

  • Identification information — name, address, date of birth, Social Security number, government ID
  • Financial information — account numbers, balances, transaction history, credit history
  • Contact information — email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Device & usage data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, time on site
  • Location data — general location when you use mobile services (with your permission)

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Open and manage your accounts and process transactions
  • Verify your identity and prevent fraud
  • Send you account alerts, statements, and legal notices
  • Personalise your online banking experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Improve our products and services

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us deliver our services (under strict confidentiality agreements)
  • Affiliated companies such as Merrill Lynch and other Bank of America subsidiaries
  • Regulatory and law enforcement authorities when required by law
  • Credit bureaus as permitted under the Fair Credit Reporting Act

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our privacy team or call us at 1-800-432-1000.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to recognise your browser, remember your preferences, and analyse site traffic. You can control cookie settings through your browser. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of Online Banking.

We also use web beacons in our email communications to understand how you interact with our messages.

Children's Privacy

Our online services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

Online Banking Service Agreement

By enrolling in and using Online Banking, you agree to the Online Banking Service Agreement. Key points include:

  • You are responsible for keeping your User ID and Password confidential
  • You must notify us immediately of any unauthorised account access
  • Electronic records are the official record of your Online Banking activity
  • The agreement is governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina

The full agreement is available on request at any branch or by calling 1-800-432-1000.

AdChoices

We participate in interest-based advertising programs. This means we may show you ads on other websites based on your browsing activity. You can opt out of interest-based advertising by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out portal or by adjusting your browser settings.

Accessible Banking

Bank of America is committed to making banking accessible to everyone. Our accessibility features include:

  • Screen-reader compatible Online Banking and Mobile App
  • Accessible ATMs with audio and braille keypads
  • Large-print and audio statements on request
  • TTY/TDD telephone service at 1-800-288-4408
  • Branch accessibility features including ramps, reserved parking, and assisted service

To request an accommodation, please visit your nearest branch or call 1-800-432-1000.
